# PSU carrier PCB – specification (v0.2)

KiCad 9 project: `kicad/psu_carrier.kicad_{pro,sch,pcb}`, generated by `tools/` (see
"Regenerating"). Netlist: `netlist.yaml`. Fab outputs for PCBWay: `fab/` (see `fab/README.md`).
3D: `../cad/vendor/psu_carrier.step`.

## Board
- 42 × 44 mm, corner radius 1.5 mm, 2-layer FR-4, 1.6 mm, 1 oz Cu, HASL lead-free or ENIG.
  (v0.1 was 40 × 36: too small for the 37 mm IRM-03 plus the 2 mm rail keep-outs and the
  primary parts.)
- Origin = board centre. Board coordinates: X right, Yup toward the rocker, viewed from the
  **F (component) side**. KiCad's aux/grid origin is at the board centre.
- **All parts on the F side, which faces into the box.** The B side is flat (THT leads only)
  and faces the plate.
- Rails: no copper, pads or footprints within 2.0 mm of the left/right edges (rule areas
  `rail_left`, `rail_right`). No copper within 1.0 mm of the top/bottom edges.
- Tallest parts above the board top: PS1 15.1 mm, J1 13.8 mm. THT leads protrude up to
  1.9 mm on the B side before trimming.

## Isolation slot
Notch in the outline from the top edge (Yup = +22) down to Yup = −8 (under the module),
2.0 mm wide at X = −7.5 … −5.5 (F view), round end. The board stays one piece below
Yup = −8. The module's silkscreen body line is split where the slot passes under it.

## Layout (F view, mm)
| Ref | Position | Rot | Notes |
|-----|----------|-----|-------|
| PS1 | pin 1 (15.24, −0.61) | 270 | body X −18.5…18.5, Yup −21.5…2.5. AC pins (1, 3) top-right, DC pins (16, 14) bottom-left, NC pin 5 at (−15.24, −0.61) |
| J1  | pad 1 L' (5.08, 15), pad 2 N (0, 15) | 180 | **wire entry faces the top edge (+Yup)**, screws face the free F side. Verified from the KiCad 3D model: the entry openings are on the footprint's +y side, so 180° turns them to the top edge. |
| F1  | pad 1 (14, 17), pad 2 (14, 11.92) | 270 | TR5, vertical |
| RV1 | pad 1 L_F (13.5, 4.5), pad 2 N (8.5, 6.6) | 180 | |
| C1  | + (−12, 9), − (−14, 9) | 180 | |
| J2  | pad 1 +5V (−12, 17), pad 2 GND (−14.5, 17) | 180 | vertical header; plug from the F side |

Primary (mains) is the right half of the top strip; secondary (5 V) is the left half.
L and N never cross: L' → F1 → PS1.1 runs down the right edge on F.Cu; N runs through the
middle on B.Cu.

**Mounted in the plate** (F side into the box, Yup toward the rocker) the board is seen from
its B side from the room. In plate coordinates it is rotated 180° about the Y axis, so plate
X = −board X: the slot is at plate X +5.5…+7.5, J1 at plate X 0…−5.08, J2 at plate X +12…+14.5.

## Circuit
J1 (L', N) → F1 (T1A time-lag) on L' → RV1 across L–N after the fuse → PS1 IRM-03-5
AC inputs. PS1 +Vo/−Vo → C1 (47 µF) → J2 (5 V, GND) → lead to MSR-2 5 V/GND.

## Isolation (targets – human review required)
Enforced by `kicad/psu_carrier.kicad_dru` with netclasses Primary (/L_IN, /L_F, /N) and
Secondary (+5V, GND):
- Primary ↔ secondary clearance ≥ 6.0 mm and creepage ≥ 6.0 mm. KiCad 9 `creepage`
  constraint, which routes around the slot.
- N ↔ L_IN/L_F clearance and creepage ≥ 2.5 mm. J1 pads were reduced to 2.4 mm because the
  stock 2.6 mm pads leave only 2.48 mm.
- Primary copper ≥ 1.0 mm from any board edge; all copper ≥ 0.5 mm.
- The rules were checked to fire on deliberately broken copies of the board.

These are design targets, not a compliance claim. Verify them against IEC 62368-1
(reinforced, 250 VAC working, PD2, OVC II) before fab. PS1 itself is rated 4.2 kVAC
I/P–O/P per its datasheet.

## Open items for human review
1. **F1 rating.** T1A was chosen by the designer, not taken from Mean Well. The IRM-03 inrush
   is 20 A typ at 230 VAC. Check the I²t against the Littelfuse 392 curves and your breaker.
2. Confirm the approvals you need on F1, RV1 and J1 (CSA/UL listings) for a BC permit.
3. Isolation targets above against IEC 62368-1 tables.
4. 5 V lead from J2: use wire insulated for ≥300 V (docs/SAFETY.md rule 4).

## Regenerating
```
pcb/tools/export_fab.sh        # regenerate libs, schematic, board; ERC + DRC; all fab outputs + STEP
```
Individual steps (system `python3` has pcbnew; the 3D envelope needs the CAD venv):
`gen_3d.py` (TR5 fuse envelope, KiCad ships none), `gen_libs.py`, `gen_project.py`,
`gen_schematic.py`, `gen_pcb.py`, `gen_bom.py`. Edit `tools/parts.py` and re-run instead of
hand-editing the KiCad files. Hand edits in KiCad are fine too, but they are overwritten
the next time the generators run.
