## PCB order settings

- Size 47 × 72 mm, 2 layers, FR-4, 1.6 mm.
- Copper weight: open, see the thermal review below (1 oz as designed, 2 oz option).
- Surface finish HASL lead-free or ENIG. Solder mask and silkscreen on both sides.
- **The outline has a 5 mm routed slot** from the bottom edge up under PS1. It is part of
  Edge.Cuts, not a drill. Add "routed isolation slot in outline, do not omit" to the order
  remarks.
- Four 2.6 mm mounting holes, all unplated (NPTH drill file), with no copper under the posts.
- No castellations, impedance control or V-cut.

## Assembly

- Front (F): F1, RV1, C1, J2 (SMD, reflow). Back (B): J1, PS1 (THT).
- **S1 is not fitted by PCBWay.** The rocker snaps into the printed insert from the front
  first, then the board goes onto its pins from behind and S1 is soldered by hand.
- PS1 sits flush on the back. IRM-03 soldering limits per its datasheet: wave 265 °C 5 s,
  hand 390 °C 3 s.
- J1 wire entries face the board's top edge (towards S1), away from PS1.
- Source exactly the BOM MPNs for S1, J1, PS1, F1 and RV1 (safety-critical; no substitutes
  without review). C1 may be any 22 µF 10 V X5R 1206.

## Human review before ordering

- [ ] F1 rating T1A is the designer's choice; Mean Well gives none (inrush 20 A typ at
      230 VAC). Its 200 A breaking capacity at 250 VAC needs review against the branch
      circuit's prospective fault current.
- [ ] Isolation targets in `../kicad/wiring_board.kicad_dru` (D-24: L–N, L in to switched
      L, mains to PE ≥ 3.5 mm; mains to 5 V ≥ 8.0 mm plus the slot) reviewed against IEC
      60664-1 / 62368-1 and UL/CSA 62368-1.
- [ ] Thermal review of the 15 A paths (narrowest 1.3–1.8 mm between J1's pads, D-37); choose
      the copper weight.
- [ ] J1 pin rows (8.2 mm apart, from WAGO's 3D model, D-37) checked on a part.
- [ ] S1 pin numbering checked with a meter on a sample (D-26).
- [ ] Approvals of S1, J1, F1 and RV1 adequate for the BC homeowner permit and inspector.
- [ ] Gerbers opened in a viewer (PCBWay's online viewer is fine): the slot is present.
